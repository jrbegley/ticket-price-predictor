Decision to be made:

Given the time between now and the concert, should I buy a ticket now, or wait for the price to drop?

Price to be tracked:

Cheapest 3 listings for 1 ticket, including fess, for a GA floor ticket. This helps normalize prices based on seats, etc. 

Expected finding:

Prices for low-demand shows are lowest right before showtime, while prices for high-demand shows slowly climb over time up until the event date

Metric:

Average % overpay compared to the lowest possible price that a show reached

Baseline:

Price of purchasing the day before
Price of purchasing when tickets first release

Target:

Future minimum price as a ratio of the current price, e.g. minimum/current -> if the ratio < 1, then you should wait, if not, then buy. 

Factors to consider:

Time Until Show
Venue capacity divided by average wikipedia pageviews
Number of listings divided by venue capacity
Price change over past week
Current price relative to listing after 1 week of ticket release
